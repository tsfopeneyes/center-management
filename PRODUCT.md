# Product
<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->
## Platform
web
## Users
HAIFN introduction: teenagers passing the center who scan a QR code on their phones. Center operators answer visitor questions through shared management DM.
## Product Purpose
Introduce what HAIFN is, its operator, representative activities, school worship community, and welcome visitors to the second floor.
## Capabilities and Constraints
Existing React, Vite, Tailwind and Supabase project. Six introduction screens at /welcome, next/previous controls and scroll transitions. Visitor chat must remain connected to the existing management DM. Preserve all approved Korean information. Hosting deployment requires explicit user instruction.
## Brand Commitments
HAIFN red and existing warm cream background; app font consistency. Use provided actual characters. No star beside HAIFN wordmark. The user explicitly authorizes complete replacement of the welcome composition, typography treatment and layout while retaining content and brand colors.
## Evidence on Hand
src/pages/HaifnIntroduction.jsx contains approved content. public/brand/center contains actual group, Hati, Pongpong, Cloudy and Didi/Dum assets. Foundation description and partner names were supplied by the user. No fabricated additional claims.
## Product Principles
Communicate to passing teenagers quickly. Make each screen a coherent and impactful poster. Keep the practical second-floor invitation clear. Keep trust information and chat available.
